Practical maths for smarter farming decisions

Created by Kartik & Tushar

Welcome to our combined project!

This website combines crop recommendation with fuel, water, electricity and farming-cost calculators. Enter your own values to explore how mathematics can help estimate farm expenses.

Important: Results are educational estimates, not official agricultural advice. Crop suggestions are simplified and the regional data is limited.

🌾 1. Crop Recommendation

Your crop suggestions will appear here.
Simplified model: Crop score = 0.7 × Season match + 0.3 × General suitability. This is a learning model, not a scientific prediction.

⛽ 2. Tractor Diesel Cost

Estimated diesel cost will appear here.
Formula: Hours × litres per hour × price per litre.

💧 3. Irrigation Water Estimate

Estimated water use will appear here.
Formula: Litres/hour × hours/session × number of sessions.

⚡ 4. Irrigation Electricity Cost

Estimated electricity cost will appear here.
Formula: Pump power × hours × price per kWh.

🧮 5. Total Farming Cost

Enter your estimated expenses. Include diesel/electricity here only if you want the total to include them.

Estimated total cost will appear here.

📊 6. Compare Two Farming Plans

Your comparison will appear here.
Difference = Higher estimated cost − Lower estimated cost.
